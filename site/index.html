How the score works. Each of the four factors is normalised across the listings that pass your filters, lower being better, then combined with your weights. It is a ranking within this set, not an absolute rating — change the filters and every number moves. A listing with no square footage is scored worst on price per sqft rather than best, which is the honest treatment of missing data.

About the data. These listings are invented. Their prices, square footage and property-tax rates are calibrated to real Long Island distributions — effective tax runs 1.3–1.7% of price here, which is why tax outweighs fuel in almost every row — but no address, listing or agent on this page is real. The scoring engine is the same one the private pipeline runs against live listings.
